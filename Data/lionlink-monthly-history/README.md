# LionLink monthly history

Fictional exercise data for eight selected buses, covering 24 complete months from October 2024 through September 2026. These are not full-fleet totals.

- [Monthly vehicle history](monthly_vehicle_history.csv): 192 records containing matched-period costs, maintenance counts, vehicle use and hold hours.
- [Repair spending by bus](repair_spend_by_vehicle.csv): the same repair figures grouped into two twelve-month periods.
- [Selected component observations](selected_component_observations.csv): twelve dated visits, included within the monthly totals.
- [Coverage and field definitions](data-dictionary.md): units, keys, missing values and the relationship to existing October extracts.

Vehicle identifiers match the existing Engineering and Operations records. Monthly summaries and selected visits are different views of the same history, so their charges and usage must not be added together. The supplied October job and journey extracts remain separate, more detailed records. Monthly repair jobs do not establish breakdowns, cancelled departures or passenger delay.
