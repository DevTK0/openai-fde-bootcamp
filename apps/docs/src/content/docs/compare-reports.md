---
title: Compare report periods
description: Keep the vehicle, service, and time coverage consistent when comparing figures.
---

Use this procedure when you need to decide whether a measure changed between two
periods. Start with the records and coverage notes for both periods.

1. **Define the question.** Specify the measure you want to compare. Total repair
   spending and repair cost per kilometre answer different questions.
2. **Match the population.** Use the same vehicles or services in both periods.
   If membership changed, report that difference alongside the result.
3. **Match the time coverage.** Compare complete periods of equal length. For
   operating records, check the days of the week and scheduled departure windows.
4. **Use the same definition and unit.** Check which costs or events are included.
   Keep quoted prices separate from incurred spending and plans separate from outcomes.
5. **Account for activity.** Compare repair charges with recorded kilometres, for
   example. Use a denominator from the same population and period as the numerator.
6. **State the result with its scope.** Include the population, periods, unit, and
   source. Describe gaps that limit the comparison.

Do not add totals with overlapping coverage. Monthly records and an annual summary
may describe the same spending. If the available records cannot support a matched
comparison, describe the periods separately.

See [Measures and coverage](/docs/measures/) for definitions and
[the maintenance comparison](/docs/maintenance-findings/) for an example.
