title: Overview
description: An introduction to LionLink Web.
---

LionLink Web helps you explore bus fleet operations, maintenance, incidents, and
operating costs. It brings fleet records and reports together so you can compare
performance and investigate the data behind a finding.

The [operations planning explanation](/docs/operations-planning/) traces how the planner combines resource schedules, written requirements, and AI comparisons. It covers the tuning failures, concrete recovery examples, and the paired Astra evaluation.
