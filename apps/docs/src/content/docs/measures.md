## Maintenance measures

The historical reports use these measures:

| Measure                   | Meaning                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recorded maintenance cost | Repair charges plus routine service and additional preventive work.                                  |
| Repair cost per 1,000 km  | Repair charges divided by kilometres recorded for the same vehicles and period, multiplied by 1,000. |
| Corrective repairs        | Recorded repair jobs, not the number of vehicles repaired.                                           |
| Vehicle unavailability    | Vehicle hold hours recorded for repairs or scheduled maintenance. Not passenger delay time.          |

The history covers eight selected vehicles from October 2024 to September 2026.
Monthly records, annual summaries, and selected work orders can overlap.
All monetary figures are SGD excluding tax.

## Operating measures

The operating reports use these measures:

| Measure                               | Meaning                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Early departure                       | Departure before its scheduled time.                                                                                  |
| Departure within the five-minute band | Departure from zero to five minutes after its scheduled time, inclusive.                                              |
| Late departure                        | Departure more than five minutes after its scheduled time.                                                            |
| Mean arrival delay                    | Average recorded arrival delay, in minutes.                                                                           |
| 90th-percentile arrival delay         | The value at the 90th percentile of recorded delays. It shows the slower end of the distribution, not the worst trip. |
| Boardings                             | Boarding events. A person who boards more than once can appear more than once.                                        |
| Calls with a remaining queue          | Stop calls at which people remain waiting after boarding.                                                             |
| Departures at capacity                | Stop calls where the departing onboard count equals the recorded capacity.                                            |

The extract covers scheduled departures from 06:00 to 11:59 on ten weekdays,
5–16 October 2026. Complete downstream calls for those journeys are retained.
It is not an all-day or continuous service record. Dates and times are Singapore
local time.

## Planning and cost options

Workshop requests and proposed allocations are plans. Tentative completion times
are not confirmed Engineering releases. A quoted price is not incurred spending
or an approved purchase.

## Evidence limits

The supplied records are fictional exercise data.

The current reports do not establish that an intervention caused a change,
increased customer retention, or will produce a guaranteed saving.
