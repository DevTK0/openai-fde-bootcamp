These are two complete October–September years, not a partial year against a full year.

## Repairs are only part of maintenance spending

Routine servicing remains a separate cost. The total maintenance figure also
includes additional preventive work. Adding repair charges to the total maintenance
figure would count those repairs twice.

## More distance explains only part of the change

Recorded distance increased from **{fmt(maintenance.earlierKm)} km** to
**{fmt(maintenance.latestKm)} km**, a change of
**{distanceGrowth}%**.
Repair charges grew faster than this measure of activity.

<FindingFigure
  client:load
  {...repairRateChart}

The rate is a comparison of recorded cost and use. It does not explain whether
vehicle age, component condition, workshop practice, or another factor caused
the increase. Those questions require the repair and condition records.

## The sample covers selected vehicles

Do not extend this result to all operating vehicles. The maintenance sample covers
eight selected buses. The monthly ledger, annual repair summaries, and selected
visits overlap and must not be added together.
