The extract contains **{fmt(operations.metrics.trips)} journeys**.
**{fmt(operations.metrics.onTimeDepartures)} departures** fall between the scheduled
time and five minutes late. **{fmt(operations.metrics.lateDepartures)}** leave more
than five minutes late.

## Read the departure band as a defined measure

The five-minute band is a counting rule. It does not mean every journey
in that band is acceptable for every passenger, or that the bus stays on time
after leaving its origin.

## An average can hide the slower journeys

The mean recorded arrival delay is **{fmt(operations.metrics.meanArrival, 2)} minutes**.
The 90th percentile is **{fmt(operations.metrics.p90Arrival, 2)} minutes**.
These measures answer different questions: the mean describes the average,
while the percentile describes the slower end of the recorded journeys.

<FindingFigure
  client:load
  {...arrivalChart}
  source="Source: recorded arrival delays, grouped by date. The lines show the mean and 90th percentile in minutes. Negative values, if present, mean early arrival."
/>

A service-level or day-level average can still conceal a particular late journey.

## What this sample does not establish

Only departures scheduled from 06:00 to 11:59 are included. Their full downstream
journeys are retained, but this is not evidence for every evening or weekend trip.
The extract covers ten weekdays in October 2026 and cannot establish a long-term trend.
