The collections below describe different populations and kinds of evidence.
Their totals are not interchangeable.

| Source                                  | Coverage                                                                                                     | What it supports                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Monthly vehicle history                 | Eight selected vehicles, October 2024–September 2026.                                                        | Comparisons of recorded costs, distance, and vehicle unavailability.                       |
| Repair summaries and work orders        | Annual summaries and selected maintenance visits.                                                            | Detail about repair activity; coverage can overlap the monthly history.                    |
| Operating journey and stop-call records | Departures scheduled from 06:00 to 11:59 on ten weekdays, 5–16 October 2026, with downstream calls retained. | Departure timing, arrival delay, boarding activity, and recorded queues within that scope. |
| Passenger accounts                      | Six selected accounts received by phone or through the web.                                                  | Descriptions of particular experiences and identifiers for possible journey matches.       |
| Workshop planning records               | Proposed requests, resource capacity, and service-cover constraints.                                         | Assessment of whether proposed work fits the available resources.                          |
| Cost proposals                          | Quoted packages with specified work, exclusions, and validity periods.                                       | Comparison of proposed prices and scope; not actual spending or measured savings.          |

## Evidence limits

All business records are fictional exercise data. Dates and times use Singapore
local time. Costs are SGD excluding tax.

The maintenance sample does not represent every operating vehicle. The operating
extract is not a continuous, all-day service history. Selected passenger accounts
do not establish a network complaint rate.

A plan is not a completed job, and a quote is not an approved purchase. These
records alone do not establish causal effects, customer retention, or guaranteed savings.
