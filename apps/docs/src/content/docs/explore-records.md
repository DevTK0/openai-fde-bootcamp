---
title: Trace a finding to its records
description: Check the population, source values, and record matches behind a claim.
---

Use this procedure to check whether the available records support a finding.
Start with the claim and its source citation.

1. **Identify the source.** Note the collection, reporting period, and population.
   [Evidence sources](/docs/applications/) describes the supplied collections.
2. **Identify the relevant records.** Match vehicle identifiers for maintenance
   questions. For journey questions, match service, date, stop, and journey time.
3. **Distinguish scheduled and observed values.** A scheduled departure describes
   the plan; an actual departure describes the recorded event. Do not substitute
   one for the other when matching a passenger account to a journey.
4. **Check the calculation.** Confirm that each included record belongs to the
   stated scope. Check the unit and denominator against
   [Measures and coverage](/docs/measures/).
5. **Check for overlap and gaps.** Do not count an annual summary again alongside
   its monthly records. Do not treat a missing value as zero without source evidence.
6. **Record what remains unresolved.** Cite the records that support the finding
   and identify any missing evidence needed to answer the question.

A matching record can locate an event without explaining its cause. Keep the
observed result separate from any proposed explanation.
