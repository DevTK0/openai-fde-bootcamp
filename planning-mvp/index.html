About this training snapshot

Passenger counts, schedules, buses and crew in the LionLink records are synthetic exercise data. This prototype uses a bounded morning sample to illustrate planning logic; it is not a live forecast or a validated dispatch recommendation. Route and stop geography is sourced separately. Confirm local rules, data quality and service targets with the client before piloting.

EVIDENCE & ASSUMPTIONS

Data scope

Supplied evidence: operating candidate files contain directional routes, stop sequences, vehicle capacities and release windows, crew duties, timetables, trips, and passenger queue / origin arrival records. The operating records are synthetic and cover ten fictional weekdays, 5–16 Oct 2026.

Map overlays: crowd figures use the average recorded queue before and after boarding on the selected date and animate as the bus reaches each stop. The lateness ring aggregates earlier supplied stop calls at that route position and measures the share arriving at least one minute after schedule. This is an observed lateness indicator, not a breakdown probability.

Illustrative logic: hourly demand is a roll-up of five-minute origin-arrival batches for the selected date and route. Remaining-queue pressure uses recorded route-position cohorts. Bus requirements use route cycle time ÷ headway, rounded up. Scenario adjustments, event lifts, changed frequency, maintenance downtime, connection delay and cost are editable assumptions.

Not established by this dataset: full-network demand, reliable future forecasting accuracy, exact bus/driver availability for proposed plans, passenger journey outcomes, route-linked breakdown risk, real operator costs, or the client’s service thresholds. The allocation options are planning scenarios for review.

Use planning-workflows.md in the project folder for the complete four-scenario flow reference.
